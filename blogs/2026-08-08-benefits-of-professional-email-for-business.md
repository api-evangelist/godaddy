How important is it to implement professional email for your business? A professional email for business is a branded email address that uses your company’s domain name- such as name@yourcompany.com- rather than a free provider like Gmail or Yahoo. Think about how much time people spend in their inbox: 172 minutes daily checking their personal emails.
