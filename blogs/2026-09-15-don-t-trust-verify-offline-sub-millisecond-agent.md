An identity is useless if you can’t verify it. Agent Name Service (ANS) gives every agent an identity it can verify, a SCITT receipt tied to a transparency log, a status token that expires, and a proof of possession that bridges identity to OAuth 2.0. All of it verifiable offline, in fractions of a millisecond.
